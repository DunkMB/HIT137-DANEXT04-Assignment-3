Puzzle.py Instructions
1	Run the file via VSCode
2	Select your preferred grid size, either 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, 7x7 or 10x10 by selecting from the drop down Grid menu.
3	Select your image from a local folder by clicking the Load image button.
4	Relocate the tiles in the right image by left clicking on them and then the location where you want to place them.
5	Rotate the tiles by right clicking on them.
6	Mirror the tiles by Shift-left clicking them.
7	Once the tile is in the correct location and facing the correct way, a green tick will appear.
8	If you are stuck with which tile to move, then press the Hint button. You have 3 hints.
9	If you cannot solve the puzzle, then press the Solve button to see the re-assembled image.
10	A timer will tell you how long it is taking to solve the puzzle.
11	To re-try, just select another image.
12	Enjoy.
